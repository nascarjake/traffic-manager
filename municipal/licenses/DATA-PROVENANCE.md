## City of Chicago

- Title: Average Daily Traffic Counts (dataset gc7y-n4xa)
- Creator/copyright holder: City of Chicago (copyright status not specified in dataset metadata).
- Source: https://data.cityofchicago.org/d/gc7y-n4xa
- Download endpoint: https://data.cityofchicago.org/resource/gc7y-n4xa.json
- Query: $where=midpointlat between 41.902 and 41.918 AND midpointlon between -87.688 and -87.666; $order=timestamp DESC; $limit=80
- Files used: public/data/chicago-counts.json
- Modifications: none to downloaded rows. Runtime matches road names and proximity within 450 m; uses the most recent first for identical proximity. Counts retain dates/directions. Duplicated segment observations do not multiply calibration intensity.
- License/permission: publicly downloadable government dataset, subject to Chicago data portal terms at https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/narr/foia/data_disclaimer.html. An explicit standalone license/version was not specified in retrieved metadata; unknown. Review before a public commercial release.
- Interpretation: daily directional camera-derived counts, not live counts and not measured turning movements. An assumed 8% peak-hour share feeds a bounded educational demand rate. Destinations and hour-of-day profiles are synthetic. Some observed camera segments may be adjacent to the playable boundary.

## 2026-10-29 control and district update

The original bundled extract checksum is unchanged: all traffic-control nodes in this retained source were already referenced by roads. The preparation script now explicitly retains standalone `highway=traffic_signals`, `highway=stop`, and `highway=give_way` nodes too. Runtime mapping preserves the direction relative to the original OSM way through graph simplification. Directional approach signs are distinguished from central all-way stops; unknown control types are labeled model assumptions. Public imports use bounded 900 m tiles, merge duplicate OSM IDs, retain requested bounds and clip roads at those bounds. Timing programs and live lamp colors are simulated, not downloaded municipal signal plans.

Tag references: https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:highway%3Dtraffic_signals ; https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:highway%3Dstop ; https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:highway%3Dgive_way . All new building, foliage, contact-shading and sign geometry is generated by original project code; no third-party assets were added or replaced.
