MODEL PROVENANCE
SIMULATION BOUNDARIES

Road geometry, mapped buildings, and available one-way tags come from OpenStreetMap. Building heights use mapped values or an estimated 2–4 storeys. Façades, rooftop details, trees and parked cars are artistic dressing; park outlines use mapped areas where available.

Trips are weighted toward housing, shops and workplaces. Peak-hour shares, destinations, turn movements and daily demand patterns are modeled assumptions.

This educational vehicle model simplifies conflict handling and includes simplified pedestrians and excludes transit priority, parking maneuvers, emergency vehicles, surveyed road grades and signal warrant analysis. It is not a validated engineering tool.

How this test works

Repeatable synthetic traffic, same starting clock and routes. A wave replaces ambient traffic temporarily; Return restores your district. Cleared means the full approach-to-exit trip is finished. At-entry cars are waiting to spawn safely. Replay resets the wave using your draft timing, even if paused or queued. Live retiming or adding pedestrians makes that run exploratory. No ambient pedestrians; crossing calls remain available. Each test ends when all cars clear or at 3 simulation minutes.
