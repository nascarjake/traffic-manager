SIMULATION BOUNDARIES

Road geometry, mapped buildings, and available one-way tags come from OpenStreetMap. Building heights use mapped values or an estimated 2–4 storeys. Façades, rooftop details, trees and parked cars are artistic dressing; park outlines use mapped areas where available.

Trips are weighted toward housing, shops and workplaces. The peak-hour share (8%), destinations, turn movements and daily demand pattern are modeled assumptions.

This educational vehicle model simplifies conflict handling and excludes pedestrians, transit priority, parking, emergency vehicles, road grades and signal warrant analysis. It is not a validated engineering tool.
